How to pick keywords
  • Recommended picks are pre-selected — start there for the best return.
  • High-intent tags mark searches with emergency or "ready to book" energy. These convert best.
  • Use phrase match for service-led terms ("brake repair brisbane"), broad for symptoms ("car won't start") so close variants get captured.
  • Pick fewer keywords with stronger intent over a long list — quality always beats quantity on a local campaign.
Why negatives matter
  • Every irrelevant click costs money. A solid negative list typically saves 15-30% of monthly spend.
  • We've grouped them by reason — forbidden services (air-con, motorbikes), DIY searchers, jobseekers, competitor brands, wrong locations.
  • Recommended ones are pre-ticked. Untick only if you specifically want those searches.
Targeting the right suburbs
  • 42 suburbs across East & Southeast Brisbane plus the Springwood / Logan core — these are where you can respond fastest.
  • Google Ads also targets "people regularly in these locations" by default, so a Sunnybank resident at work in the CBD still sees the ad.
  • Untick anywhere you don't want to travel. Less radius = better CPC and faster jobs.
